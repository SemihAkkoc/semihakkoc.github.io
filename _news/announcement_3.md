New preprint: [On the Diverse Dynamical Behaviors Arising in Deep Linear Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18584), which shows that in two dimensions, stacked linear self-attention reduces exactly to a generalized Kuramoto model. Under review at _Foundations of Computational Mathematics_.
